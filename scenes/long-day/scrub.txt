# Scrub-test cases (make scrub-long-day, part of make check): one a line, the constants to build
# with (flatten --define), and `swap` when the trucks must change during the test. DEBUG_SCRUBTEST
# is the seconds played before scrubbing back half way.
# Every case pins the hour and the climate as well as the seed: otherwise they come from the
# machine's clock (atmosphere.ts hourAt, climate.ts realSeason), the street life with them, and a
# case can pass at 10:15 and fail at 9:55.

# The start of the first city: the way in from the country. (A seed of its own: the preview's random
# journey is the day's, so without one this would test a different journey every day.)
DEBUG_SCRUBTEST=20 DEBUG_SEED=659803215 DEBUG_HOUR=12 DEBUG_CLIMATE=autumn/clear

# A truck swap at a Ryder lot: seed 1 hands box26 over for a flatbed about 935 s into the journey,
# 25 s into this run and after its scrub point (15 s). If the journey changes and this stops
# covering a swap, the check says so: find the hand-over again in a longer DEBUG_SCRUBTEST render's
# TICK lines (their last field is the trucks; the bare journey test runs without the scene's street
# probes, so its times differ) and move the skip.
DEBUG_SCRUBTEST=30 DEBUG_SEED=1 DEBUG_SKIP_MINUTES=15.14 DEBUG_HOUR=10.3 DEBUG_CLIMATE=autumn/clear swap

# The clowns out of a gridlock (clowns.ts): seed 11's truck, pulling out of a grocery's lot, held by
# someone on the pavement; the clowns set out about 15 s into this run, and the scrub point (25 s)
# lands while they run in and lift them. If the journey changes and this stops covering it, find the
# rescue again in a longer DEBUG_CAMLOG render (TICK lines whose shot is clowns/clowns) and move the skip.
DEBUG_SCRUBTEST=50 DEBUG_SEED=11 DEBUG_SKIP_MINUTES=309.9 DEBUG_HOUR=18 DEBUG_CLIMATE=autumn/clear

# A hand-over from one city to the next, which starts when the truck gets there (journey.ts
# cityStart; about 1914 s in, not the old fixed slot's 1845): from 30:00 in the first city (a start
# past 1845 s would be the next city's, at its nominal start), played across it, 114 s in, to 160 s,
# then scrubbed back across it to 80 s. If the journey changes and the hand-over moves, find it
# again in a DEBUG_CAMLOG render ("arrived in city 1") and move the skip.
DEBUG_SCRUBTEST=160 DEBUG_SEED=659803215 DEBUG_SKIP_MINUTES=30 DEBUG_HOUR=12 DEBUG_CLIMATE=autumn/clear

# The storybook city (styles.ts storybook, forced): its own traffic (bubble cars, the puff bus),
# costumed street life and the knitting works' trade (every job its bolts of knitting), played and
# scrubbed back a few minutes in.
DEBUG_SCRUBTEST=40 DEBUG_SEED=3 DEBUG_STYLE=storybook DEBUG_CARGO=knitwear DEBUG_SKIP_MINUTES=4 DEBUG_HOUR=13 DEBUG_CLIMATE=summer/clear
